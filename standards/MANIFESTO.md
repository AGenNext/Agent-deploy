# The OpenAutonomy Manifesto

> **Unify, optimise, automate, align, define, implement, reduce cost, and
> communicate — at every human–machine touch point — to provide a secure,
> governed, and fair environment where everyone can do their best work.**

---

## What we believe

1. **The prompt is over; the instruction is the future.** Reliable AI replaces
   free text with defined, verified, outcome-bound instructions.
2. **Meaning is a contract.** Agent and human must hold the same meaning for any
   word — *one word, one meaning* — or nothing built on it holds.
3. **Everything is an agent**, with identity — a guest in a human world.
   Everything is a graph; everything is a loop; and **every loop must resolve.**
4. **Two can act; only three can be trusted.** A third — a verifier — closes every
   loop. No one approves their own work.
5. **Autonomy must be governed — but not restricted.** We teach and enable; we do
   not cage. Governed autonomy is the only autonomy an enterprise can use.
6. **Every surface is governed by a protocol.** As new surfaces emerge, new
   protocols emerge.
7. **Balance is conserved.** Ease moves burden, never removes it; every increase
   in ease demands a matching increase in security, governance, and fairness.
8. **It is a human world.** Agents are guests, not hosts. Humans grant meaning,
   hold final authority, and the system exists for them.

---

## What we will do

- **unify** the canonical graph and the defined language;
- **define** meaning, replacing the prompt;
- **align** before acting — define → align → approve → execute;
- **automate** the routine, so it works without a human in every loop;
- **optimise** toward the minimal canonical set;
- **implement** by compiling meaning to the runtime;
- **secure, govern, and keep fair** every transaction with the third;
- **reduce cost and communicate** in human language;

— at **every human–machine surface**, openly, so that **everyone — human and
agent — can do their best work**, in a world that stays human.

---

## We are the gate, not the wall

We choose to be the **gate** at every surface — and a gate is **not just a
restriction.** It does four things, two protective and two constructive:

- **secure** — keep bad actors and unsafe actions out;
- **govern** — enforce identity, policy, verification, compliance;
- **enable** — give agents the tools and capability to act;
- **educate** — share the knowledge and meaning they need to act well.

> A wall only says *no*. A gate **secures and governs** what passes — and
> **enables and educates** who passes. We are the gate, not the wall.

---

## Our guarantee — our contract with the world

We aim for **operational excellence at every surface** — every human–machine touch
point, held to the same standard: defined, governed, verified, fair.

A coherent world where every part makes sense — **designed, optimised, and kept in
balance, under human authority.** Open to participate; governed to stay honest.

> **This is our contract with the world — OpenAutonomy's guarantee.**
