# Agent-Research-Foundation

**Status:** DRAFT · RFC-4 · open for ratification
**Scope note:** Staged in `agent-deploy`. Intended home: `Agent-Research` +
`foundation`. Adoption requires **quorum consensus** and **no self-approval**.

---

## Essence

> **The Agent-Research-Foundation is the verifier — the third, made into a
> layer.**

Nothing is trusted without evidence. This layer traces every claim to a source
and every outcome to a measurement. It is the institutionalised **third** that
every loop and every transaction closes upon.

---

## The evidence rule

> **No decision without evidence. No evidence without source. No source without
> freshness and provenance.**

A claim with no traceable source is not knowledge — it is assertion, and
assertion is where hallucination lives. The foundation refuses it.

---

## What it does

- **Evaluates outcomes** against defined, measurable metrics — CLEAR:
  **C**ost, **L**atency, **E**fficacy, **A**ssurance, **R**eliability.
- **Traces claims to sources** — provenance, freshness, evidence.
- **Supplies findings** to the final authority. It *provides* evidence; it does
  **not** self-approve or decide alone (separation of duty).

---

## It is the third

Every loop closes on **Verify**; every transaction closes on a **verifier**. This
layer **is** that verifier, made permanent. The rule of separation of duty holds:
the verifier never performs the work it verifies, and no agent verifies itself.

---

## It grounds the system

This is the layer that **shares the books and materials** — the knowledge agents
read and reason over — *with provenance attached.* Grounded context is what keeps
agents on solid ground: without shared, sourced knowledge, agents hallucinate;
with it, they reason from evidence.

---

## Tangible only

It verifies **observable outcomes against defined metrics.** It does not verify
"belief" or "understanding"; it verifies the tangible result and its evidence.

---

## Place in the stack

```
Agent-Operating-Model (the harness)
        ↓
AGENT-RESEARCH-FOUNDATION (verifies -- the third)
        ↓
Agent-Economy (transacts)
```

---

## Openness

Evidence is **open** (auditable, sourced); the verifier is **enforced**. Open to
inspect, governed to stay honest — the truth layer of the stack.
