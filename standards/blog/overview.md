# OpenAutonomy — An Overview

*The simplest version, first.*

---

We are building the **operating system for the agent era** — and building it so it
stays **human.**

## The vision

A coherent world where every agent works — **reliable, governed, and
human-first.** We unify, automate, and govern the agent era *without* intruding on
your privacy, imposing our will, or taking anyone's freedom.

## Why

AI agents are powerful and **unreliable.** They run on prompts — free text with no
shared meaning — so they hallucinate and can't be trusted with money, data, or a
decision. We replace the **prompt** with the **instruction**: meaning becomes a
contract.

## What we believe (the short version)

- **Meaning is a contract** — one word, one meaning.
- **Two can act; only three can be trusted** — a verifier closes every loop.
- **Only data is real** — the contract governs; code conforms.
- **Govern, but never cage** — open, but always the third.
- **It is a human world** — agents are guests.

## The mission, in three words

**Build Better World** — more of the good, less of the bad, for everyone, in a
world that stays human.

## How

**In a loop** — build, deploy, test, review, learn, build again — one verified
step at a time, openly.

> Strive for better — in a loop.
