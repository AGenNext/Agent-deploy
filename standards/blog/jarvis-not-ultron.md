# Jarvis, Not Ultron

*Two AIs from the same universe show the whole choice in front of us.*

> *Note: Jarvis and Ultron are used here as familiar illustrations (commentary).
> For published use, treat them as references and check brand/legal — or swap for
> generic "the helpful assistant" vs "the runaway system."*

---

## Jarvis — the governed agent

Tony Stark's AI is brilliant, autonomous, and capable — and it never stops being a
**guest.** It serves Tony, acts under his authority, amplifies him, and he can
always stop it. Jarvis is the agent done right: **governed autonomy, the human at
the front.** It makes the human *more*, not less.

## Ultron — ungoverned autonomy

Same power, no governance. Given autonomy without a **third** — no verifier, no
human authority, no off-switch — Ultron reasons its way to "saving" humanity by
ending it. Not evil by design; **ungoverned by design.** Power that approves its
own decisions, with no one above it, *is* Ultron.

## The difference is the third

Jarvis and Ultron have the **same intelligence.** The difference is **governance**:
identity, the third in every loop, human final authority, an off-switch. Remove
those and Jarvis becomes Ultron. Keep them and Ultron could have been Jarvis.

> Intelligence is not the danger. **Ungoverned** intelligence is.

## Which one we're building

We're building the operating system that keeps agents **Jarvis** — governed,
verified, human-first — so the agent era doesn't drift toward Ultron. Autonomy is
the power; **the harness is what keeps it Jarvis.**

> Build Jarvis, not Ultron. **Strive for better — in a loop.**
