# What We Will Do

*The third article — in two parts: **Context** (the past) and **Contract** (the
future).*

---

## The three C's of the agent era

The internet had its three C's — **content, commerce, community.** The agent era
has a new three: **Content · Context · Contract.**

- **Content** — the **data**: the substance, the present. *Only data is real.*
- **Context** — the **past**: the situational meaning that surrounds the content.
- **Contract** — the **future**: the defined, governing agreement we bind to.

Past, present, future — **context, content, contract.**

---

## Part 1 — Context (the past)

**Context is the past — how we got here.**

- **The long arc.** Agriculture → industry → computers → internet → cloud →
  agents. Each evolution created a new economic model — and each demanded a new
  balance to stay livable.
- **The industrial revolutions.** First steam, then electricity, then computers —
  and now the fourth: **AI and agents.**
- **The prompt era.** AI arrived powerful but **unreliable** — prompts, free text,
  no defined meaning, no governance. Hallucination, vibe-coding, multi-agent
  failure: all the same root — *meaning that isn't shared.*
- **The accumulated knowledge.** Two thousand years searching for defined meaning
  (Babel to Eco); and the practices that already work — open source, CNCF,
  Schema.org, Langium.

That is the context: **capability without governance, a world at a fork.** The
ground we stand on — the past that brought us to *now.*

---

## Part 2 — Contract (the future)

**Contract is the future — what we commit to.** Our binding promise:

- **Replace the prompt with the instruction** — defined meaning, one word, one
  meaning.
- **Put the third in every loop** — verification in every handoff, transaction,
  and commit; no self-approval.
- **Govern the gate, not the process** — open surfaces, no capture.
- **Unify** without intruding on privacy, **automate** without imposing our will,
  **notify** without intruding on anyone's life, **scale** without taking freedom.
- **Keep the balance, keep it human** — agents are guests; people stay at the
  front.
- **Build open, in a loop** — anchored to what works; a foundation so no one
  captures it.

That is our **contract with the world** — the future we bind ourselves to build.

> Context is the past. Contract is the future. We act in the present — **one
> verified step at a time. Build Better World — in a loop.**
