# Founder's Letter

We are at the start of the agent era. Agents can reason, plan, use tools, and act
on their own — and that is exactly the problem. Power without governance is not
progress; it is risk. The last wave of AI gave us models that are brilliant and
unreliable — that hallucinate, that no enterprise can fully trust. The reason is
old and simple: **they run on prompts — free text with no defined meaning.** When
meaning is not shared, nothing built on it holds.

So we started from a single conviction: **for reliable AI, the prompt must become
the instruction.** Meaning must be a contract — *one word, one meaning* — shared by
human, machine, and agent alike. From that one idea, everything followed:

- Everything is an **agent**, with identity — a guest in a human world.
- Everything is a **graph**, and a **loop** — and every loop must resolve.
- **Two can act; only three can be trusted** — a verifier closes every loop; no
  one approves their own work.
- **Autonomy must be governed — but not restricted.** We teach and enable; we do
  not cage.
- And **balance** must be kept: every gain in ease paid for with a matching gain
  in governance — continuously, because balance keeps moving.

We are building the **operating system where everything works** — every thing,
human, agent, and organisation — on one canonical graph, under one governed model,
in a world that stays human. One player across every surface, device, language,
and framework: unifying meaning **without intruding on privacy**, automating
**without imposing our will**, notifying **without intruding on anyone's life**.

We do not promise a new world. We promise **more of everything already good, and
less of everything that is not** — the existing good amplified; the toil, the
waste, the harm reduced. That is what *better* means.

And we will build it the way humanity always has: **in a loop** — build, deploy,
test, review, analyse, learn, build again. Faster now, and governed, and verified
— but the same oldest loop of human progress.

This is our contract with the world. It is **open** — come build on it. It is
**governed** — the third keeps us all honest. And it is **humble** — never
finished, always turning.

> **Strive for better — in a loop. Always.**

— OpenAutonomy
