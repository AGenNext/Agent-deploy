# Agent Theory — everything is an agent

**Status:** DRAFT · foundational note (the entity layer).

---

## The trinity of foundations

Three views of one system, completing each other:

| theory | claim | what it gives |
|---|---|---|
| **Agent theory** | everything is an **agent** | the **entity** (the node, with identity) |
| **Graph theory** | everything is a **graph** | the **structure** (nodes, edges, meaning) |
| **Loop theory** | everything is a **loop** | the **motion** (must resolve) |

> The agent is the **node**. The graph is how agents **relate**. The loop is how
> agents **act and resolve**.

---

## The canonical entity

> **Everything is an agent. The agent is the canonical entity — the fundamental
> unit of the system.**

The nodes of the graph are agents; the loops are agents acting. An agent is the
one unit that:

- holds **identity**,
- perceives state,
- always has a **next step** (acts),
- runs a **loop that must resolve.**

Just as every *word* has one canonical *meaning*, every *agent* has one canonical
**identity**. One identity, one entity.

---

## An agent must have identity

To be an agent — a canonical entity — a thing **must have identity**:

- **unique** — one identity, one entity; no duplicates, no ambiguity;
- **attributable** — every action traces back to it;
- **canonical** — governed, single source of truth, change-controlled.

> **No identity → not an agent.** Identity is the price of entry.

(This is the AOM Identity layer made foundational: *every action must be
attributable.* Without identity there is no accountability, no trust, no agent.)

---

## The three worlds are three agents

Human · machine · agent — all are **agents**, each with its own identity, meeting
on the graph and sharing canonical meaning.

- **Human** — the agent that grants meaning and holds final authority.
- **Machine** — the agent that executes deterministically.
- **Agent** — the agent that reasons between them.

Three agents, one shared ground (the graph), one canonical meaning per word.
The triad again: three meet, and the meeting is what makes coordination possible.

---

## Lifecycle, respect, responsibility

An agent is a canonical entity — so, like any responsible participant:

- **Its lifecycle must be managed and governed.** Draft → Tested → Approved →
  Production → Observed → Evaluated → Optimised → Retired — every stage gated. An
  agent is not spawned and forgotten; it is *managed across its whole life.*
- **It is given equal respect and carries equal responsibility.** With identity
  comes both **standing** and **accountability**. An agent is treated as a real
  participant (respect) *and* is answerable for its actions (responsibility) — the
  two are inseparable. No respect without responsibility; no responsibility
  without identity.

---

## It is a human world

The final frame, which bounds everything above:

> **It is a human world.**

Everything is an agent — *operationally.* But the world the agents serve is
**human**: the human grants meaning, holds **final authority**, and is the one
the system exists for. Agents participate as responsible, respected entities —
**under human authority, never above it.**

"Everything is an agent" is the *model*; "it is a human world" is the *purpose.*
The first never overrides the second. This is the guardrail against the
overclaim: agents are first-class participants, **not sovereigns.** Identity,
respect, responsibility, and a governed lifecycle — all in service of a human
world.

> **Agents are the guest, not the host.**

A guest is welcomed, respected, and given standing — *and* a guest follows the
house rules, under the host's authority, in the host's home. Respect and
responsibility, both, **because it is not their house.** The human is the host;
the world is the human's home; the agent is the honoured guest.

---

## Honest scope

Operationally, this system models **every actor and entity as an agent** — a
canonical entity, with identity, that perceives and acts. That is true *by
construction* and is what the standards depend on.

The broader claim — *everything in reality is an agent* — has a real theoretical
lineage (the free energy principle: any persisting thing with a boundary / Markov
blanket can be **described** as an agent minimising surprise). But we **claim only
the operational version** and admire the rest as hypothesis. Build on what you
can verify; do not mistake the hypothesis for a proof. No Babel overclaim.
