## Verification studies

We reproduce and stress-test the science our system rests on. Code in
[`experiments/`](../../experiments).

- **Active inference & artificial reasoning** (Friston et al.) — *reproduced.*
  Expected information gain **over models** (via Bayesian Model Reduction) makes
  rule discovery more sample-efficient; all four directional predictions hold.
- **Integrated information, proxies vs. approximations** (Barrett et al.) —
  *tested.* Integrated-information proxies are **not** approximations of Φ — they
  move in opposite directions under the same parameter change.
