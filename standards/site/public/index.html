The operating system for the agent era — governed, verified, human-first.

Frontier research that puts people at the front.

The problem

AI agents are powerful and unreliable. They run on prompts — free text with no shared meaning — so they hallucinate and can't be trusted with money, data, or a decision. The bottleneck was never capability. It's trust.

You cannot trust what you cannot define, verify, or govern.

What we believe

  • Meaning is a contract — one word, one meaning.
  • Two can act; only three can be trusted — a verifier closes every loop.
  • Only data is real — the contract governs; code conforms.
  • Govern, but never cage — open, but always the third.
  • It is a human world — agents are guests.

The mission, in three words

Build Better World.

More of the good, less of the not — for everyone, in a world that stays human. Research · Product · Proactive all converge on that one line.

How

In a loop — build, deploy, test, review, learn, build again. One verified step at a time, openly.
