## The problem

Agents are powerful — and unreliable. They run on **prompts**: free text with no
defined meaning. So they hallucinate, drift, and can't be trusted with money,
data, or a decision. **You cannot govern what you cannot define.**

## What we do

Provide the **right information**, with the **right context** and **intent**, at
the **right time**, to the **right entity** — and make sure it has the tools,
skills, and knowledge to do its best, within the known constraints.

The prompt becomes the **instruction**. Meaning becomes a **contract**.

## How

- **One word, one meaning.** Defined, not described.
- **Two can act; only three can be trusted.** The third closes every loop.
- **Govern the gate, not the process.** Open surfaces; no capture.
- **Only data is real.** The contract governs; code conforms.
- **Balance, kept dynamically.** In a human world.

## The guarantee

A coherent world where every part makes sense — **designed, optimised, and kept in
balance, under human authority.** Open to participate; governed to stay honest.
