# The Foundation Principle

**Status:** DRAFT · the root · everything derives from this.

---

## The root

> **Two can act; only three can be trusted.**

Two parties cannot, on their own, share meaning, close a loop, or be trusted — a
**third** (a contract, a verifier, a witness) is what makes shared meaning and
trust possible. From this single root, everything else follows:

- **meaning** needs a third — the interpretant (the contract; *one word, one
  meaning*);
- **trust** needs a third — the witness (no self-approval);
- **a loop** needs a third — the verifier (every loop resolves, closed by the
  third).

Define meaning with the third; act one step at a time; close every loop with the
third; keep the balance. That is the whole system, unfolded from one principle.

---

## The Foundation's charter

The **OpenAutonomy Foundation** holds the open standard in trust, so no one
captures it. It commits to:

- **Open** — open standards, **open surfaces**; anyone may participate.
- **Neutral** — owned by no single vendor; **the third is distributed.**
- **Governed** — quorum consensus, no self-approval, change-controlled meaning
  (`language_change_quorum`).
- **Federated** — each field sovereign; canonical within, federated across.
- **Balanced** — every ease offset by governance; balance kept, dynamically.
- **Human** — a human world; agents are guests; human final authority.
- **Anchored** — build on what exists (open source, CNCF, Schema.org, Langium);
  innovation is the next edge.

## One concept, many names

"Foundation principle," "constitution," "charter," "canonical design principles" —
**these all name the same canonical thing**: the governing rules. To honour *one
word, one meaning* (and avoid duplicate docs), they resolve to a **single
canonical owner** — `Agent-Constitution` — and every other document **references**
it, never copies. The same rule we apply to repos: **one owner; the rest
reference.**

---

## Why a foundation

A **foundation**, not a company, holds the open standard — so no single party can
capture it. As CNCF holds cloud-native, the **OpenAutonomy Foundation** holds the
agent standard: neutral, open, governed, in service of a human world.

> The root is the third. The foundation keeps it open. **Strive for better — in a
> loop.**
