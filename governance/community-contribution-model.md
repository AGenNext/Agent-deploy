# Community Contribution Model

**Status:** DRAFT · owned by `Agent-deploy`.

> How the community — **human and agent** — contributes: open to all, governed at
> the gate, recognised fairly. Anchored to the open-source model.

---

## Who contributes

Anyone — **human or agent.** Both are treated as agents (Agent Theory): each with
**identity, respect, and responsibility**, each a guest in a human world.

---

## Roles

- **Contributor** — proposes and builds (human or agent).
- **Reviewer / Maintainer** — the **third**; reviews and approves (**no
  self-approval**).
- **Steward / Quorum** — governs the standard and meaning; ratifies
  (`language_change_quorum`).

---

## The contribution flow (anchored to open source)

```
issue / RFC → claim → develop → PR → review (the third) → gate → merge → recognise
```

1. **propose** (issue / RFC) — define intent + outcome *before* building;
2. **claim** — one contributor per task (no overlap);
3. **develop** — follow the Agent-Development / Agent-Coding protocols
   (recommended, not coerced);
4. **submit** (PR) — small, focused, defined;
5. **review** — a maintainer / third approves; no self-approval;
6. **gate** — `Agent-deploy` validation (commit/release checks, boundaries,
   meaning);
7. **merge** — only on green + approval;
8. **recognise** — credit the contributor.

---

## Recognition & value (the economy)

Contributions create value; value is **recognised and rewarded**:

- **attribution** — identity; every contribution is attributable;
- **reputation / trust** — a trust score that grows with **verified**
  contributions;
- **credits** — Agent-Economy (agent / marketplace credits) for value delivered.

**Effort ↔ reward must balance** (Balance Theory): contribution is rewarded in
proportion to **verified** value — no more, no less.

---

## Federation & sovereignty

Each community or field runs its **own** contribution process and recognises its
**own** contributors. Conformance is to the **open standard at the gate**, never a
mandated internal process. Contributors keep **sovereignty** over their own work.

---

## Open + governed = balance

Anyone may contribute (**open**); the third validates (**governed**); value is
recognised fairly (**balanced**). The **gate, not the wall.**

> Contribution is a loop: *propose → build → review → merge → recognise →
> propose.* Each turn, the project gets better. **Strive for better.**
