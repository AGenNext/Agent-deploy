# Glossary — canonical terms

**Status:** DRAFT · one word, one meaning · governed by `language_change_quorum`.

> Every word used in these documents resolves here, to **exactly one** meaning.
> Where a term sounds intangible, the definition is its **tangible proxy**.

---

| term | canonical meaning |
|---|---|
| **attribution** | the binding of a contribution to the **identity** that produced it — *who* did it. About **origin and accountability**. |
| **credit** | the **recognition / value** awarded to an attributed contribution — *what it earned*. About **reward**, in proportion to **verified** value. |
| **contributor** | an agent (human or machine) that proposes or builds a contribution. |
| **contribution** | a **defined, verifiable** unit of work submitted to a project (a PR, an instruction, a document). |
| **reviewer / maintainer** | the **third** that reviews and approves a contribution; **cannot approve its own**. |
| **steward / quorum** | the body that governs meaning and **ratifies** changes (no self-approval). |
| **the third** | the **verifier** that closes a loop or transaction; nothing self-approves. |
| **gate** | the **surface** where contributions are validated before acceptance; it **secures, governs, enables, educates**. |
| **recognition** | the act of **attributing** and **crediting** a contribution. |
| **trust score** | a measure of an identity's **verified contribution history**; gates how far past *now* it may act unsupervised. |
| **outcome** | the **defined, verifiable result** a contribution is contracted to produce; measured on CLEAR. |

---

## The one distinction to keep clear

- **Attribution = who** (origin, accountability).
- **Credit = reward** (value earned, in proportion to verified outcome).

They are **not** the same word, and never share a meaning. One word, one meaning —
always.
